💬 Idioms

Well-being & Relaxation Idioms

Seven idioms you can use in real conversations. Each includes meaning, example, and Arabic hint.

😌 Take it easy

Meaning: Relax and not work too hard

Example: "You look exhausted — take it easy this weekend."

Arabic: خفّف على نفسك / ارتاح

🔋 Recharge your batteries

Meaning: Rest so you get your energy back

Example: "I need a short holiday to recharge my batteries."

Arabic: يشحن طاقته تاني

🌬️ Clear your head

Meaning: Stop worrying and think more calmly

Example: "He goes for a walk to clear his head after work."

Arabic: يصفّي ذهنه

💨 Let off steam

Meaning: Release stress or anger in a healthy way

Example: "Playing football helps me let off steam."

Arabic: يفضفض / يخرّج الطاقة السلبية

🤒 Under the weather

Meaning: Feeling slightly ill or low

Example: "She’s a bit under the weather today, so she stayed home."

Arabic: تعبان شوية / مش على بعضه

😴 Sleep on it

Meaning: Wait until tomorrow before making a decision

Example: "Don’t decide now — sleep on it and tell me tomorrow."

Arabic: فكّر لحد بكرة قبل ما تقرر

🪶 Take a load off your mind

Meaning: Stop worrying about something

Example: "Finishing the exam really took a load off my mind."

Arabic: ارتاح من همّ كان شايله

Gerunds & Infinitives — Deep Dive

📊 Grammar Slides

🎯 A gerund is a verb used as a noun

When you add -ing to a verb, it starts behaving like a noun.

SentenceWhat the gerund does
Reading is a great way to relax.subject of the sentence
Gardening is a good hobby.subject of the sentence
Focus on your breathing.after a preposition

✏ The 4 Spelling Rules for -ing

RuleExamples
1. Most verbs: just add -ingread → reading • talk → talking
2. Ends in -e: remove the edance → dancing • ride → riding
3. Ends in -ie: change to -ylie → lying • tie → tying
4. Short verb (consonant-vowel-consonant): double the last letterrun → running • hit → hitting • sit → sitting
But be careful: not every verb doubles. begin → beginning, prefer → preferring, forget → forgetting — BUT listen → listening, visit → visiting, enjoy → enjoying.

🎯 Which verbs take which form?

Always verb + ingAlways to + verbEither form is fine
enjoy, avoid, practice, dislike, keep, suggest, considerwant, need, hope, decide, plan, learn, promisestart, like, love, prefer, begin, continue

I enjoy watching TV. • I need to start yoga classes. • I like walking = I like to walk.

❌ The Negative Infinitive

To make an infinitive negative, put not BEFORE to.

PositiveNegative
I decided to go to work.I decided not to go to work.
Try to miss fewer classes.Try not to miss your next yoga class.

🚨 THE BIG MISTAKE

Arabic speakers often put "to" after verbs that need -ing, because Arabic uses أن + verb almost everywhere.

❌ I enjoy to read before bed.  ❌ I avoid to eat late.  ❌ I suggest to take a break.
✅ I enjoy reading before bed.  ✅ I avoid eating late.  ✅ I suggest taking a break.

Memory hook: enjoy, avoid, practice, dislike, suggest — these five NEVER take "to". Say them out loud until they sound wrong with "to".

📖 Reading

Keeping Your Mind Healthy

Having a healthy mind is important for feeling good. A good mind helps us think clearly, feel nice, and deal with stress better. When we take care of our mental health, we enjoy life more and have better relationships with others. Here are some activities to keep your mind healthy:

1. Exercise: Moving your body is a great way to feel better. You can do any activity you like, such as walking, yoga, or dancing. Exercise helps to reduce stress and makes you feel happy.

2. Take a Bath: A hot bath is a nice way to relax after a long day. The warm water helps relax your muscles.

3. Read a Book: Reading is a great way to relax. Find a book you enjoy and read for at least 15 minutes a day. Reading helps your mind relax and can make you focus better.

4. Meditate: Find a quiet place, close your eyes, and focus on your breathing. Try to meditate for 5 to 10 minutes each day.

5. Do Puzzles: Puzzles like crosswords or Sudoku are fun and good for your brain.

6. Garden: Gardening is a good hobby that can make you feel better. Spend time outside in the sun, growing flowers or vegetables.

7. Be Thankful: Counting the things that make you happy can improve how you feel. Each day, write down three things that make you want to say "thank you."

8. Write Your Ideas: Writing can help you understand your thoughts and feelings.

9. Spend Time with Friends: When you feel sad or alone, spending time with someone you trust can make you feel better. If you want to meet new people, consider joining a new class.

10. Clean and Organize: A clean and organized place makes you feel calm.

Note for Q2: video games are never mentioned in this list.

🎭 Roleplay

Roleplay Scenarios

🎤 Scenario 1: The Well-being Podcast

  • One student is the podcast host
  • The others are mental health experts
  • The host asks questions and comments on the advice
  • The experts suggest practical ways and explain why they work

Host: Welcome to the show! Today we’re talking about mental health. What do you recommend?

Expert 1: I always suggest starting with exercise. Walking for 20 minutes helps to reduce stress.

Host: That’s interesting. Why walking specifically?

Expert 1: Because it’s easy and free! You don’t need to join a gym.

Host: And what about you?

Expert 2: I recommend writing your thoughts every evening. It helps people understand their feelings.

Host: Great advice. Thank you both for joining us today!

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Scenario 2: Helping a Stressed Friend

  • Your friend is stressed about exams
  • Suggest relaxation activities and explain why they help
  • Use: suggest + ing, try + ing, need to + verb

Friend: I’m so stressed. I can’t sleep and I can’t focus.

You: That sounds hard. Have you tried taking short breaks while studying?

Friend: Not really. I just study for six hours straight.

You: I suggest studying for 45 minutes, then walking for 10. It really helps to clear your head.

Friend: That might work. What else?

You: Try not to use your phone before bed. And you need to sleep at least seven hours.

Friend: Thanks — that takes a load off my mind.

🏥 Scenario 3: Asking a Doctor for Advice

  • You feel tired and stressed all the time
  • The doctor asks about your habits and gives advice
  • Practice gerunds after "avoid / suggest / recommend"

Doctor: How have you been feeling lately?

You: I feel under the weather. I’m tired all the time.

Doctor: Do you exercise?

You: Not really. I dislike going to the gym.

Doctor: That’s fine. I recommend walking for 30 minutes a day instead.

You: I can do that. Anything else?

Doctor: Avoid drinking coffee after 6 p.m., and try to practice breathing techniques before bed.
