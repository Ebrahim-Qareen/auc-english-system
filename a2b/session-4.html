💬 Idioms

Places & Destination Idioms

Seven idioms you can use in real conversations. Each includes meaning, example, and Arabic hint.

🪨 A stone’s throw away

Meaning: Very close, a short distance away

Example: "Our hotel was a stone’s throw away from the beach."

Arabic: قريب جدًا / على بعد خطوات

🏜️ In the middle of nowhere

Meaning: Very far from any town or city

Example: "The camp was in the middle of nowhere — no shops, no signal."

Arabic: في مكان بعيد خالص

❤️ The heart of the city

Meaning: The central, busiest part of a city

Example: "Our flat is right in the heart of the city, near everything."

Arabic: قلب المدينة / وسط البلد

💎 A hidden gem

Meaning: A wonderful place that few people know about

Example: "That small café in Alexandria is a hidden gem."

Arabic: مكان جميل مش مشهور

🐝 The hustle and bustle

Meaning: The noisy, busy activity of a place

Example: "I love Cairo, but sometimes I need a break from the hustle and bustle."

Arabic: زحمة ودوشة المدينة

📸 Picture-perfect

Meaning: So beautiful it looks like a photograph

Example: "The blue streets of Chefchaouen are picture-perfect."

Arabic: جميل زي الصورة

🗺️ Put a place on the map

Meaning: Make a place famous

Example: "The new museum really put the town on the map."

Arabic: يخلي المكان مشهور

📊 Grammar Slides

1 / 1

⭐ The Basic Order: O-S-S-A-C

When you use more than one adjective before a noun, English follows a fixed order. Memorize O-S-S-A-C.

#CategoryWhat it describesExamples
1Opinionyour personal feelingbeautiful, ugly, delicious, lovely
2Sizehow big or smallhuge, tiny, big, small
3Shapethe form of itround, square, long
4Agehow old or newancient, new, old, young
5Colorthe colorblue, red, pink, black
Example: "We took the big (size) old (age) black (color) train." • Limit: use 2–3 adjectives before a noun, never more.

🔗 The "and" Rule

When two adjectives come after the verb be (at the end of the sentence), put and between them.

Before the noun (NO "and")After "be" (USE "and")
a big old black trainThe house is small and old.
a lovely new red carThe house is large and white.
amazing new phonesThe bookshop has old and new books.

🧭 Describing Where a Place Is

PhraseExample
located inParis is located in France.
onGiza is on the Nile River.
in the north / south / east / west ofAlexandria is in the north of Egypt.
on the coast ofAlexandria is on the coast of Egypt, on the Mediterranean Sea.

🚨 THE BIG MISTAKE

In Arabic, adjectives come after the noun and their order is flexible. In English the order is fixed and they come before the noun.

❌ a red big car  ❌ an Egyptian old beautiful city  ❌ The house is small old.
✅ a big red car  ✅ a beautiful old Egyptian city  ✅ The house is small and old.

Quick check: Is the adjective before the noun? Then no "and", and follow O-S-S-A-C. Is it after is / are / was? Then use "and".

Special Places to Visit

Six cities from around the world, each special for a different reason.

🇪🇪 Tallinn, Estonia — a city on the Baltic Sea. It is the perfect place for people who enjoy historical places as well as modern tourist activities.

🇱🇦 Luang Prabang, Laos — located in the north of Laos. It has beautiful temples and is known for its quiet atmosphere. Tourists love it because it is quiet and has a relaxing atmosphere.

🇺🇾 Colonia del Sacramento, Uruguay — this city is not big, but it has a rich history.

🇲🇽 San Cristóbal de las Casas, Mexico — a city in North America. It is the best place for people who like to buy souvenirs and traditional outfits.

🇲🇦 Chefchaouen, Morocco — a city in the middle of the mountains with beautiful blue buildings. One feature that makes it attractive is the friendly people.

🇳🇨 Nouméa, New Caledonia — this city has beautiful nature and oceans and is perfect for people who like water activities.

🎭 Roleplay

Roleplay Scenarios

📺 Scenario 1: The TV Commercial

  • In a group, advertise a destination like Hurghada or Sharm El-Sheikh
  • Describe the location, what makes it special, and the activities
  • You are the voice actors — 2 minutes

Voice 1: Are you tired of the hustle and bustle of the city?

Voice 2: Come to Hurghada! It is located on the coast of Egypt, on the beautiful Red Sea.

Voice 1: Enjoy the warm sunny weather all year round.

Voice 2: You can go diving, swimming, or just relax on the quiet golden beaches.

Voice 1: Our hotels are modern and comfortable, and they are a stone’s throw away from the sea.

Voice 2: Hurghada — your picture-perfect holiday. Call us today!

🏠 Scenario 2: Describing Your Hometown

  • A foreign friend asks about your hometown
  • Describe the size, the atmosphere, and the weather
  • Use the correct adjective order

Friend: So, where are you from exactly?

You: I’m from Alexandria. It’s located in the north of Egypt, on the coast of the Mediterranean Sea.

Friend: What’s it like?

You: It’s a big historical city, but it’s not as crowded as Cairo. The weather is warm and sunny most of the year.

Friend: What can you do there?

You: You can visit the Library of Alexandria, walk along the Corniche, and eat amazing fresh seafood.

Friend: It sounds like a hidden gem!

✈️ Scenario 3: Dream Holiday Destination

  • A street interviewer asks about your dream destination
  • Say where it is and why you want to go
  • Use location phrases and at least 3 adjectives

Interviewer: Excuse me, what’s your dream holiday destination?

You: I’d love to visit Japan.

Interviewer: Why Japan?

You: Because I want to discover a completely different culture. Tokyo is a huge modern city, but Kyoto is quiet and historical.

Interviewer: What would you do there?

You: I’d visit the ancient temples and try the local food. The natural scenes there are breathtaking.
